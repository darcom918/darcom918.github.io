Youth in Action · Host organisation · Păulești, Prahova

Encouraging young people to take part in developing social entrepreneurship initiatives

Photo album Video feedback

2013Youth in Action

Description

This project was funded by the European Commission through the “Youth in Action” programme, Action 1.1 – Youth Exchanges.

The team was made up of 28 young people aged 18–30 (including 6 group leaders) from the 5 partner countries.

For 7 days (not counting travel days), the young people took part in various non-formal education activities aimed at: exchanging experience, learning and focusing on concrete activities to promote social entrepreneurship, and generating new ideas in the field of social entrepreneurship.

The main objectives of this youth exchange were:

  • Raising young people’s awareness of the need to develop and carry out activities in the field of social entrepreneurship
  • Raising awareness of the possibility of using the products of social entrepreneurship in the non-formal education of disadvantaged young people

One of the main aims of the project was to develop ideas for volunteering activities that can be run during the Non-formal Education Days in each of the 5 participating countries, and to organise an activity plan that the applicant carried out in a youth centre in Bucharest on one of the 2 days dedicated to Non-formal Education (12.10.2013).

As for the direct results of this project, we can mention intangible results such as greater awareness among young people of the need to develop and carry out social entrepreneurship activities, and greater awareness of the possibility of using the products of social entrepreneurship in the non-formal education of young people with fewer opportunities. Direct tangible results were also achieved, such as the activity plan developed for the applicant to carry out, and the toys made during the exchange activities.
