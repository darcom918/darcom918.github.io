Description

Asociația “Inițiative Sociale”, together with its transnational partners Hellenic Youth Participation (Greece), Scambieuropei (Italy), Europejska Fundacja Edukacyjno-Sportowa (Poland), Asociacion DESES-3 (Spain) and European Youth Bank (Turkey), announces the completion of the project “Entrepreneurial Ideas”, a project funded by the European Commission through the “Erasmus +” programme.

The project “Entrepreneurial Ideas” was designed by and for young people, to encourage cultural interaction, personal development and cooperation.

The direct beneficiaries of this project were 50 participants (43 young people aged 18–30 and 7 group leaders with no age limit) from the 6 partner countries. The youth exchange activities took place over 11 days (including travel days) and used non-formal education methods, aimed at: more involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, exchanging knowledge and experience, learning and developing concrete activities or ideas to promote entrepreneurship, developing creativity and developing competences in activities that involve creating business plans.

At the same time, through a constant intercultural exchange of ideas between organisers, partners and participants, the common goal they worked together for was achieved, and the project reached its objectives, namely:

  • Raising the awareness of the 43 participants and 7 group leaders about the need to promote and develop an entrepreneurial spirit in today’s economic context;
  • Raising the awareness of young people from the 6 participating countries about the importance of young entrepreneurs and their role in shaping the future of European society;
  • Encouraging the participants to develop entrepreneurial ideas by involving young people with fewer opportunities;
  • Creating business plans (start-ups) in a multicultural space;
  • Developing and encouraging solidarity and tolerance among young people, especially to strengthen social cohesion in the EU.
  • Creating a network for future cooperation between the partners of this project.

Moreover, the project’s results speak for its impact:

  • a strategic partnership between the 6 promoters, who will join forces again for future projects;
  • the participants’ motivation and wish to write and implement similar projects, starting from ideas developed during this youth exchange;
  • more active involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, which leads to improved European standards;
  • the intercultural exchange, the exchange of national values and above all the exchange of ideas, which supported intercultural dialogue and ties;
  • 8 business plans started/developed by the participants during the activities;
  • 1 video used to inform about and promote the project in particular and the Erasmus + programme in general;
  • 1 project blog; it is a platform for information and presentation, where visitors can read about the project in detail and also see photos and videos from the activities;

Among the project’s measurable indicators, we can list:

  • more than 700 visitors to the project blog; visitors who could/can read about the aim and objectives of this project, see photos and videos from the activities, and also read the young participants’ testimonials.
  • informing more than 200 young participants of other youth mobility projects, both in Romania (in other projects run by the host organisation, in high schools, in universities etc.) and abroad (in youth exchanges, training courses and partner networks), about the aim, objectives and activities of this project.
  • informing more than 18,000 people from Romania, Greece, Italy, Poland, Spain and Turkey (the countries the partners/participants of this project come from) about the aim, objectives and activities of this project in particular, and about the aim and objectives of the “Erasmus +” programme in general. This information and promotion was done through the social network Facebook.
