InnoVenture

Keywords: initiativeentrepreneurshiplabour market

Where it all began. InnoVenture laid the foundations of our Erasmus+ journey, with young people from several countries who learned to turn ideas into plans and plans into first steps towards a career.

Project blog

  • A1 Young People in Business You(th) in Business ideasteamworkbusiness Have an idea? Here you learn to plan it, test it and turn it into a real concept, together with an international team.

Keywords: communityworkpersonal path

Our most ambitious chapter so far. LevelUP brought the European experience into the heart of the local communities of Vatra Dornei, through study visits, meetings with people from the community and hands-on learning.

Project blog

  • A5 Rural Youth in Action Rural Youth in Action rural developmentinitiativecooperation The village is not a place you leave, but one you can change. Young people discovered how local initiative can transform a community.
