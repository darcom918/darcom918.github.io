Description

Asociația “Inițiative Sociale” (Romania), together with its transnational partners The Starry Start of Talents Foundation (Bulgaria), Scambieuropei (Italy), Europejska Fundacja Edukacyjno-Sportowa (Poland), Asociacion DESES-3 (Spain) and European Youth Bank (Turkey), announces the completion of the project “Start-up Machine”.

This project was funded by the European Union through the Erasmus+ programme, Key Action 1: Learning mobility of individuals – Mobility project for young people and youth workers.

The direct beneficiaries of this project were 36 participants (30 young people aged 18–30 and 6 group leaders with no age limit) from the 6 partner countries.

The youth exchange activities took place over 11 days (including travel days) and used non-formal education methods, aimed at: more involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, exchanging knowledge and experience, raising awareness of the need to develop an entrepreneurial spirit, developing creativity and developing skills and competences in the field of social entrepreneurship.

At the same time, through a constant intercultural exchange of ideas between organisers, partners and participants, the common goal they worked together for was achieved, and the project reached its specific objectives, namely:

  • SO1. Raising the awareness of the 36 participants from the EU about the need to develop an entrepreneurial spirit.
  • SO2. Increasing the knowledge and competences of the 36 participants of this youth exchange in the field of social entrepreneurship.
  • SO3. Gaining the knowledge needed to set up a social enterprise.
  • SO4. Writing business plans for setting up and developing social enterprises.
  • SO5. Encouraging and developing solidarity and tolerance among the young participants, to support social cohesion across the European Union.
  • SO6. Developing a strategic partnership between the promoters of this project.

Moreover, the project’s results speak for its impact:

  • the continuation of the strategic partnership between the 6 promoters, who will join forces again for future projects;
  • the participants’ motivation and wish to write and implement similar projects, starting from ideas developed during this youth exchange;
  • more active involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, which leads to improved European standards;
  • the intercultural exchange, the exchange of national values and above all the exchange of ideas, which supported intercultural dialogue and ties;
  • 6 social business plans (for 6 practice companies) started/developed by the participants during the activities;
  • 6 promotional videos (for 6 practice companies) made by the young participants;
  • 1 project blog; it is a platform for information and presentation, where visitors can read about the project in detail and also see photos and videos from the activities;

Among the project’s measurable indicators, we can list:

  • more than 2500 visits to the project blog; the blog’s visitors could/can read about the aim and objectives of this project, see photos and videos from the activities, and also read the young participants’ testimonials.
  • more than 1,100 views of the photo album made especially for this youth exchange (available on the host organisation’s Facebook page).
  • more than 225 views of the video clip that accompanies the press release on the completion of the mobility activities (video available on the host organisation’s Facebook page).
  • informing more than 100 young people, both in Romania (in other projects run by partners of the host organisation, in EVS volunteer groups etc.) and abroad (in youth exchanges, training courses and partner networks), about the aim, objectives and activities of this project.
  • informing more than 5,000 people from Romania, Bulgaria, Italy, Poland, Spain and Turkey (the countries the partners/participants of this project come from) about the aim, objectives and activities of this project in particular, and about the aim and objectives of the “Erasmus +” programme in general. This information and promotion was done through the social network Facebook.
