Description

Asociația INIȚIATIVE SOCIALE [AIS], together with its transnational partners SCAMBIEUROPEI from Italy, HELLENIC YOUTH PARTICIPATION [HYP] from Greece, EUROPEJSKA FUNDACJA EDUKACYJNO-SPORTOWA [EFES] from Poland, ASOCIACION DESES 3 from Spain and GAZIANTEP LOCAL YOUTH GROUP [GLYG] from Turkey, announces the completion of the project “Getting to know each other!”.

This project was funded by the European Commission through the “Erasmus +” programme, Key Action 1: Learning mobility of individuals – Mobility project for young people and youth workers.

The project “Getting to know each other!” was designed by and for young people, to encourage cultural interaction, personal development and cooperation, as a multilateral youth exchange implemented between 01.02.2018 and 31.08.2018; the mobility of this project took place in the town of Horezu, Vâlcea County, between 25.04.2018 and 05.05.2018.

The direct beneficiaries of this project were 36 young people aged 18–30 (including 6 group leaders) from the 6 partner countries. The youth exchange activities took place over 9 days and used non-formal education methods, aimed at: more involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, exchanging knowledge and experience, developing competences in activities that involve creating handmade objects, developing social and intercultural competences, and promoting creativity, traditions and national customs.

The mobility took place in Horezu, Vâlcea County, where the participants had the chance to work together, to communicate in an active and constructive way and to discover the cultural diversity of each participating country. In this way, the 36 young people were able to take part in joint activities with young people from other countries and benefited from the diverse socio-cultural experience of each sending country to create a common concept.

The specific objectives of this project were:

  • OS1: Raising the awareness of 36 young people from 6 different countries about the need to develop social and intercultural competences in today’s European context, during the mobility.
  • OS2: Gaining new key competences for 36 young people during the mobility. These will be gained/developed through non-formal education methods during the mobility.
  • OS3: The social integration of 12 young people with fewer opportunities. By taking part in the project, they will feel socially included, gain self-confidence and gain key competences that will help them in their active life in the future.
  • OS4: Promoting a sense of solidarity and tolerance among the young participants, to support social cohesion across the EU during the mobility.
  • OS5: Developing and maintaining the strategic partnership between the promoters of this project.

This project produced a series of results. There are direct intangible results, such as more active involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, which leads to improved European standards, and greater awareness of other cultures, achieved by involving young people from several countries. This project also encouraged intercultural exchange, the exchange of national values and above all the exchange of ideas, thus supporting intercultural dialogue and ties through the active involvement of all the young people in the project activities.

As for the direct tangible results, the following were achieved: the paper objects/products/ornaments made during the activities of this project and the exchange blog. Another tangible result of this exchange is a collection of 6 “how-to” tutorials or videos through which anyone interested can learn the Origami technique for creating and building each object.

The project brought changes in the participants’ way of life. They developed social and intercultural competences and were encouraged to take a more active part in volunteering in the partner associations and in the communities they belong to, to promote common values and social responsibility, and to take part in other mobilities or European projects in the future.
