Privacy policy

Information about the use of the website and data protection.

This page provides general information about how data sent by visitors may be handled when they use the website of Asociația Inițiative Sociale and choose to contact the organisation by email.

By using the website, visitors can access information about the Erasmus+ Accreditation, AIS activities, projects, results and ways to get in touch. Links to external services or platforms are subject to their own privacy policies.

If you choose to contact AIS by email, you may send data such as your name, your email address and your message. This information must be used only for communication related to the request you sent.

Data provided by email

The website may contain links to external platforms, including social networks and project resources. AIS does not control the privacy policies of these external services.

How the information provided is used

Do not send sensitive information by email that is not needed for your request. For questions about the use of data or the content of the website, use the contact address published by the organisation.
