A2InnoVenture

Description

In the first year, through InnoVenture, the Young People on the Labour Market activity supported preparation for the transition to the labour market and the development of confidence, communication and self-presentation skills.

Participants developed communication, teamwork, critical thinking, reflection, self-confidence, initiative, adaptability, intercultural cooperation and the ability to plan their educational and professional future more consciously.
