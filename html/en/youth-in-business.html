Developing Entrepreneurial Skills and Empowering Young People

From 9 to 19 November 2023, Asociația Inițiative Sociale (AIS) hosted the international youth exchange “InnoVenture: You(th) in Business” (A1) in Predeal, Romania. It was the first mobility carried out under the Erasmus+ accreditation in the field of youth (2022-1-RO01-KA150-YOU-000110034), through project 2023-1-RO01-KA151-YOU-000131090.

The mobility brought together 55 people from 7 countries (Bulgaria, Greece, Italy, Poland, Romania, Spain and Turkey): 45 young people aged 18 to 30, 8 group leaders and 2 facilitators. Inclusion was an important part of the project: 15 young people with fewer opportunities took part (33.3% of all young participants) and gained access to international opportunities for learning and personal development.

About the mobility

The main aim of the mobility was to develop an entrepreneurial spirit and to improve the knowledge, skills and attitudes young people need to start and grow a business. The project set out to promote entrepreneurship as an opportunity for professional development, to encourage initiative and creativity, to develop transversal skills and to strengthen solidarity, tolerance and intercultural cooperation.

Over 9 days of educational activities, the participants took part in an intensive non-formal education programme based on experiential learning, simulations, workshops, brainstorming, role play, debates, presentations, work in multicultural teams, reflection and participatory evaluation.

The activities included team-building and inclusion sessions (City Rally, Mission Impossible), presentations on entrepreneurship and business opportunities in the seven countries, a meeting with a young entrepreneur from the USA, and thematic workshops on generating business ideas, marketing, branding, financial planning and product promotion.

A central part of the learning process was a business development simulation, in which participants, working in multicultural teams, turned entrepreneurial ideas into business concepts, analysing the resources, customers, marketing strategies and budgets they would need.

The programme also included a cultural and educational visit to the Bran–Brașov area, intercultural activities and sessions on the Erasmus+ programme, the European key competences and the Youthpass tool.

A1 produced concrete educational results and materials, created directly by the participants:

  • 7 case studies on youth entrepreneurship in the participating countries;
  • 7 practice firms, each with its own business concept;
  • 7 business plans, with presentations and marketing strategies;
  • 7 promotional videos made to promote the practice firms;
  • 1 project brochure and 1 blog dedicated to the mobility;
  • photos, videos, articles and participants’ stories, used to promote and make use of the results.

Through these activities, the 45 young people gained practical knowledge of writing a business plan, spotting entrepreneurial opportunities, managing resources and promoting products or services.

The participants also improved their communication, leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, creativity, critical thinking, digital and English-language skills. The learning process was monitored through daily reflections and evaluations, and the skills gained were recognised through Youthpass.

A notable result of the project is that 4 young people later tried to put into practice the business plans they developed during the mobility, showing how the skills gained can be applied in real life.

The mobility was coordinated by AIS together with its 6 international partner organisations, through a structured system of planning, monitoring and evaluation.

The preparation phase included selecting and preparing the participants, signing the partnership agreements, designing the educational programme, arranging travel and accommodation at the Holiday Panoramic complex in Predeal, and preparing information and learning materials.

Management was supported by digital tools such as Google Drive, WhatsApp, Facebook and online collaboration platforms. During the mobility, meetings with group leaders, daily reflections and the mid-term and final evaluations made it possible to monitor progress, identify participants’ needs and adapt the activities.

The project also promoted environmentally responsible practices, including the use of digital materials, less plastic and sustainable travel.

The post-mobility evaluation, based on 42 questionnaires, showed a high level of satisfaction and a positive impact on personal and professional development.

Satisfaction and skills development (rating scale from 1 to 4 points, where 4 is the highest score):

  • Overall satisfaction: 3.88 out of 4 points, a very high appreciation of the Erasmus+ experience.
  • Contribution of the mobility to personal development: 3.48 out of 4 points, reflecting positive effects on self-confidence, independence and personal growth.
  • Ability to work in multicultural teams: 3.62 out of 4 points, showing better cooperation and communication between young people from different cultural backgrounds.

Positive changes and skills gained (42 respondents):

  • 97.6% of respondents (41 of 42) noticed positive personal changes as a result of taking part in the project.
  • 83.3% of respondents (35 of 42) said the activities developed their entrepreneurial skills.
  • 97.6% of respondents (41 of 42) confirmed that they felt supported by the host organisation during the mobility.

These results show the project’s contribution to developing entrepreneurial and social skills, and the effectiveness of an approach based on non-formal education and experiential learning.

Feedback from the 8 group leaders was very positive: 8/8 said they were “Very satisfied” with the mobility. All eight felt that the participants had clarified their career aspirations and goals, and six noticed better job prospects and readiness to continue education or training.

The partner organisations’ evaluation confirmed the quality of management and cooperation, with scores of 5 out of 5 for communication with partners, 4.67 out of 5 for collaboration between organisations and 4.5 out of 5 for reaching the project’s objectives.

The mobility also helped build AIS’s organisational capacity, strengthen its international relationships and grow its partner network with a new organisation, D.G.T. Polska.

The project results were promoted through the dedicated blog, social media, educational materials, information events and multiplier activities organised by the partners.

For A1, at least 8 dissemination sessions were documented in 5 countries (Romania, Greece, Poland, Spain and Turkey), with about 150 attendances. On top of these came a podcast in Italy, presentations of the project materials in educational institutions in Poland, and online promotion through the networks of the organisations and participants.

The A1 blog had more than 1,641 visitors, raising the project’s visibility and promoting Erasmus+ opportunities, non-formal education and entrepreneurship among young people.

The educational materials and resources were made publicly available on the project blog, so they can be used and built on after the mobility.

“InnoVenture: You(th) in Business” was an important first step in implementing the Erasmus+ accreditation of Asociația Inițiative Sociale, contributing to its strategic objectives on entrepreneurship, young people’s skills, social inclusion, intercultural cooperation and organisational capacity.

Through the involvement of 55 people, the 7 business concepts and related educational materials, and the positive evaluation and dissemination results, the mobility showed the value of non-formal education in preparing young people for economic and professional challenges.

The experience and results of A1 also laid the groundwork for the next activities organised by AIS under the Erasmus+ accreditation, ensuring continuity for its initiatives on youth entrepreneurship and employability.

The story continues! Together we are stronger!
