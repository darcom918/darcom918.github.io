Many ideas, one word: entrepreneurship!

IT ItalyTR TurkeyES SpainLT LithuaniaRO Romania
Project blog Video feedback

2014Erasmus+

Description

The project “Many ideas, one word: entrepreneurship” was funded by the European Commission through the “Erasmus +” programme; the youth exchange took place in Eforie Nord, between 3.10.2014 and 12.10.2014.

We also believe that this project was implemented successfully, especially thanks to the good cooperation between the partners: Scambieuropei (Italy), FuturEurope (Turkey), Association Plan Be (Spain), Youth Cooperation Center “Europroject” (Lithuania) and Asociația “Inițiative Sociale” (Romania), and at the same time thanks to the active involvement of the participants (32 young people aged 18 to 25 and 6 leaders with no age limit).

At the same time, through a constant intercultural exchange of ideas between organisers, partners and participants, the common goal they worked together for was achieved, and the project reached its objectives, as follows:

  • The participants from the 5 countries involved in this project became more aware of the importance of entrepreneurship and of promoting this concept among young people;
  • The 38 young people became aware of the need to carry out activities that develop their entrepreneurial skills;
  • The project promoted solidarity and tolerance between people, contributing to stronger social cohesion across the European Union;
  • The participants involved in this project became aware of and understood the need to involve young people with fewer opportunities in activities based on the concept of entrepreneurship.

Moreover, the project’s results speak for its impact:

  • a strategic partnership between the 5 promoters, who will join forces again for future projects;
  • the participants’ motivation and wish to write and implement similar projects, starting from ideas developed during this youth exchange;
  • more active involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, which leads to improved European standards;
  • the intercultural exchange, the exchange of national values and above all the exchange of ideas, which supported intercultural dialogue and ties;
  • 4 short videos presenting the concept of entrepreneurship as seen and understood through the participants’ eyes;
  • the project blog; it is a platform for information and presentation, where visitors can read about the project in detail and also see photos and videos from the activities;
  • flyers with information about the project.

Among the project’s measurable indicators, we can list:

  • more than 2100 visitors to the project blog;
  • more than 2500 flyers distributed both in Romania (in other projects run by the host organisation, in high schools, in universities etc.) and abroad (in youth exchanges, training courses and partner networks);
  • informing more than 100 students of the “Mircea cel Bătrân” National College in Râmnicu Vâlcea about the opportunities offered by the European Commission through the “Erasmus +” programme, and familiarising them with these opportunities;
  • informing more than 100 students of the “Mircea cel Bătrân” National College in Râmnicu Vâlcea about the aim, objectives and activities of this project.
