Description

Asociația “Inițiative Sociale”, together with its transnational partners Hellenic Youth Participation (Greece), Scambieuropei (Italy), ESN MINHO (Portugal), Asociacion DESES-3 (Spain) and Gaziantep Local Youth Group (Turkey), announces the completion of the project “Grow Your Ideas!”, a project funded by the European Commission through the “Erasmus +” programme.

The project “Grow Your Ideas!” was designed by young people, for young people, to encourage cultural interaction, personal development and cooperation by promoting entrepreneurship as a tool against youth unemployment, as well as young people’s active participation in the labour market at national and European level.

The direct beneficiaries of this project were 42 participants (36 young people aged 18–30 and 7 group leaders with no age limit) from the 6 partner countries. The youth exchange activities took place over 11 days (including travel days) and used non-formal education methods, aimed at: more involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, exchanging knowledge and experience, learning and developing concrete activities or ideas to promote entrepreneurship, developing creativity and developing competences in activities that involve creating business plans.

At the same time, through a constant intercultural exchange of ideas between organisers, partners and participants, the common goal they worked together for was achieved, and the project reached its objectives, namely:

  • OS1. Developing the entrepreneurial competences and entrepreneurial spirit of the 36 participants and 6 group leaders of the youth exchange, so that they can adapt to the labour market.
  • OS2. Raising the awareness of the 36 participants and 6 group leaders about entrepreneurial opportunities, the importance of young entrepreneurs and their role in shaping the future of European society, using non-formal methods and tools, over 9 days of youth exchange activities.
  • OS3. Developing the key competences of the 36 participants and 6 group leaders related to entrepreneurship and employment, over 9 days of youth exchange.
  • OS4. Creating concrete business plans (start-ups) in a multicultural space, over 9 days of youth exchange.
  • OS5. Strengthening intercultural dialogue with the help of the 42 participants, towards a European education based on diversity, during the project.

Moreover, the project’s (tangible/intangible) results speak for its impact:

  • maintaining the strategic partnership between the 6 promoters, who will join forces again for future projects;
  • increasing the capacity of the 6 promoters to work at European level: improving their management and organisational skills;
  • the participants’ motivation and wish to write and implement similar projects, starting from ideas developed during this youth exchange;
  • more active involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, which leads to improved European standards;
  • greater awareness of the need to develop an entrepreneurial spirit, and more knowledge and competences in the field of entrepreneurship among the participants. Through this project, the young people developed skills, attitudes and knowledge (ASK) that will later help them enter the labour market more easily.
  • overcoming cultural barriers and encouraging dialogue and intercultural exchange in the field of entrepreneurship. This was achieved through the activities run during the YE, in multicultural teams, designed to spark the participants’ interest in entrepreneurship at European level, as well as in the importance of working with others to become more effective personally and socially.
  • the intercultural exchange, the exchange of national values and above all the exchange of ideas, which supported intercultural dialogue and ties;
  • 5 business plans started/developed by the participants during the activities;
  • 1 video used to inform about and promote the project in particular and the Erasmus+ programme in general;
  • 1 project blog (www.grow-your-ideas.blogspot.com); it is a platform for information and presentation, where visitors can read about the project in detail and also see photos and videos from the activities;
