Paper Handicraft Bussiness – Winter Edition

Project blog Brochure

2013Youth in Action

Description

This project was funded by the European Commission through the “Youth in Action” programme, Action 1.1 – Youth Exchanges.

The project “Paper Handicraft Bussiness – Winter Edition” was designed by and for young people, to encourage cultural interaction, personal development and cooperation.

The direct beneficiaries of this project were 38 young people aged 18–30 (including 6 group leaders) from the 5 partner countries.

The youth exchange activities took place over 9 days (including travel days) and used non-formal education methods, aimed at: more involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, exchanging knowledge and experience, learning and developing concrete activities or ideas to promote entrepreneurship, developing creativity and developing competences in activities that involve creating handmade objects.

The main objectives of this youth exchange were:

  • Raising the awareness of the 38 participants from the European Union about the need to promote and develop an entrepreneurial spirit;
  • Informing young people from 5 different countries about the risks and benefits that can come from developing a business idea based on making paper crafts (with a winter/Christmas theme) using the Origami technique. This was possible through intercultural dialogue, activities based on non-formal education, and also through media (writing a project brochure and creating a project blog);
  • Encouraging the participants to develop activities aimed at developing entrepreneurial competences;
  • Encouraging the participants to develop business ideas by involving young people with fewer opportunities;
  • Encouraging the participants to promote and develop entrepreneurial initiatives that have an impact on vulnerable groups;
  • Developing and encouraging solidarity and tolerance among young people, especially to strengthen social cohesion in the European Union.

This project produced a series of results. There are direct intangible results, such as more active involvement of young people in the communities they belong to, which leads to improved European standards, and greater awareness of other cultures, achieved by involving young people from several countries. This project also encouraged intercultural exchange, the exchange of national values and above all the exchange of ideas, thus supporting intercultural dialogue and ties through the active involvement of all the young people in the project activities.

As for the direct tangible results, the following were achieved: the paper objects/products/ornaments made during the activities of this project, the brochure and the exchange blog.

The brochure contains innovative ideas about the creative use of various materials (such as paper, plastic, wood, glass and metal) to make decorations or other objects.

Another tangible result of this exchange is a collection of 6 teasers or short clips, filmed and edited by the participants during the exchange, promoting the objects they made in the creative workshops. Moreover, these creative workshops also produced 6 “how-to” tutorials or videos through which anyone interested can learn the Origami technique for creating and building each object.

The results – the handmade paper products, the audio-video products and the exchange brochure – were shared in the following post-exchange activities:

  • A visit to the Social Centre of the Metropolis Philanthropic Foundation in Bucharest – decorating a Christmas tree and the children’s playroom,
  • The project blog,
  • The exchange brochure was distributed/shared both by volunteers of the host organisation in Romania and by participants and volunteers of the partner organisations in their home countries.

The project brought changes in the participants’ way of life. They developed competences for generating business ideas, were encouraged to take a more active part in volunteering in the partner associations and in the communities they belong to, and are keen to promote common values and social responsibility and to take part in training courses or European projects in the future.
