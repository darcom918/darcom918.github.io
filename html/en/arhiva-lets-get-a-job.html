Erasmus+ · Host organisation · Horezu, Vâlcea

Let’s get a job!

2019Erasmus+

Description

Asociația “Inițiative Sociale”, as host organisation, announces the successful completion of the project “Let’s get a job”, funded by the European Commission through the Erasmus + programme.

The partners who contributed to the project are: The Starry Start of Talents Foundation (Bulgaria), Scambieuropei (Italy), Europejska Fundacja Edukacyjno-Sportowa (Poland), Asociacion DESES-3 (Spain) and Gaziantep Local Youth Group (Turkey).

The main activity of this project was a youth exchange with young people from 6 countries (Bulgaria, Italy, Poland, Romania, Spain, Turkey), who worked and lived together as an international group between 21 October 2019 and 31 October 2019, in Horezu, Vâlcea.

The main objectives of the project are listed below:

  • SO1. Raising the awareness of the 42 participants about the importance of young people in the labour market and their role in building a future for European society;
  • SO2. Motivating and informing the 42 participants, and offering a good space for exchanging ideas and strategies that can help them become more responsible citizens of their communities;
  • SO3. During the mobility, the 42 participants wrote Europass CVs and motivation letters that can be adapted to several kinds of jobs.
  • SO 4: During the exchange, the 42 participants learned the steps towards a successful job interview and how to improve their communication skills.

The project activities used non-formal education methods, designed to encourage the participants to be active in the labour market. Through activities such as theatre, role play, simulation, and verbal and non-verbal communication, the young people gained competences such as digital, multilingual and entrepreneurial competences, and those related to being a responsible citizen. All these competences, and others, were recognised through the Youthpass certificate, which the participants received at the end of the project.

The results of the project include its blog (www.let-get-a-job-ye.blogspot.com) and Facebook page, as well as 5 videos showing the situation of youth unemployment and presenting a few solutions for tackling this widespread problem at European level.

Another result of the project was greater tolerance between cultures and religions and more social inclusion.
