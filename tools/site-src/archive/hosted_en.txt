h1 | Young Enterprises
p | The project “Young Enterprises” is funded by the European Commission through the “Erasmus +” programme, Key Action 1: Learning mobility of individuals – Mobility project for young people and youth workers.
p | The project group was made up of 40 participants (32 young people aged 18 to 25 and 8 leaders with no age limit). For 9 days (not counting travel days) they took part in various activities using non-formal education methods, aimed at: raising awareness of the need to develop an entrepreneurial spirit, and increasing their knowledge and competences in the field of entrepreneurship.
p | Project duration: 4 August 2014 – 15 March 2015
p | Youth exchange dates: 7 November 2014 – 17 November 2014
p | Youth exchange venue: Eforie Nord, Constanța
p | The activities of this youth exchange took place in Eforie Nord, Constanța County, where the participants had a good setting for getting to know each other and working in teams to carry out activities in the field of entrepreneurship, and also had the chance to discover the cultural diversity of each country.
p | The specific objectives of this youth exchange were:
li | Raising the awareness of the 40 participants from the European Union about the need to promote and develop an entrepreneurial spirit;
li | Gaining competences, skills and knowledge in the field of entrepreneurship, and the information needed to start a business;
li | Developing the participants’ competences to understand the usefulness and importance of a business plan for setting up a company/firm/enterprise;
li | Developing and encouraging solidarity and tolerance among young people, especially to strengthen social cohesion in the European Union;
li | Gaining communication, relationship and teamwork skills.
p | Among the direct intangible results of this project, we can mention greater awareness of the need to develop an entrepreneurial spirit and new knowledge in this field, so that in the future the participants can start businesses as potential entrepreneurs.
p | At the same time, throughout this youth exchange, dialogue and intercultural exchange were encouraged in order to overcome possible cultural barriers.
